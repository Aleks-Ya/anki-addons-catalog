About

Anki Addons Catalog helps you find add-ons for Anki. Type what you need in plain words, and Claude searches the catalog, checks which addons support your Anki version and whether they are still maintained, and recommends the best fits.

Where the data comes from

  • The Anki Addons dataset: a monthly snapshot of every addon on AnkiWeb, enriched with GitHub, Anki Forum and AI-summary data.
  • The Anki Addons MCP server gives the AI read-only access to the dataset. You can connect it to your own AI assistant, too.

Caveats

  • Data is refreshed monthly, so the newest updates may be missing.
  • AI summaries and AI answers can be wrong. Check the AnkiWeb page before installing.
  • This site is not affiliated with AnkiWeb or the Anki project.

Privacy

Questions you ask are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the answer, and may be kept briefly in a cache. No account and no tracking cookies are used; a short-lived cookie remembers that you passed the bot check.
